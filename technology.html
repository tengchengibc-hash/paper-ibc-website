Technology & Patent

Seamless continuous wrap, wet-pressed inner liner and the three-layer composite board provide structural strength under liquid pressure, stacking and folding.

Seamless structure

Continuous wrap, no side seams

The sleeve is continuously wound on a mandrel from outer kraft plies and A-flute medium. With no vertical overlap, compression load spreads evenly around the wall; corner shear stress is transferred to normal compression rather than concentrated at a seam.

  • • Cross-section: octagonal, 4 faces + 4 bevelled corners
  • • Fold-flat: twin crease lines + relief cavity
  • • Assembly: no nails, no metal frame, no fumigation
Utility model patent

Foldable corner structure

The foldable corner hinge structure is protected under a Chinese utility model patent. The patent covers only the four-corner folding hinge structure; it does not claim rights over other solutions and does not guarantee equivalent protection outside China.
