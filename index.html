Why MoFort

Engineered around transport reality

📦

Seamless continuous wrap

The sleeve is continuously wound on a mandrel with no vertical overlap; corner shear stress is transferred to normal compression across the wall.

🔬

ISTA 3E tested

The 1000L packaging unit completed the ISTA 3E transport sequence at a third-party laboratory. Report No. 2026-Y009-211, 14 May 2026.

🌱

Paper-based end-of-life

The outer body enters the standard corrugated recycling stream; the liner is sorted and processed separately. Empty boxes fold flat for storage and return.

Applications
